## 🧊 ZaRemba AI Watcher 1.1 – Quarantine & bring your own AI

Found something dangerous? Now you can take it out of action with one click, ask an AI you trust to explain it, and
delete it for good – or restore it exactly as it was.

### ✨ New in 1.1
- 🧊 **Quarantine** – one click takes a malicious MCP server, agent skill or poisoned package out of action. **Restore**
  puts it back exactly; **Delete** removes it for good (two clicks, your decision only).
- ⚡ **Automatic quarantine** (optional) – known threats from the signed threat database are locked away the moment
  they are found. Switched on by *Protect everything*. Nothing is ever deleted automatically.
- 🤖 **Bring your own AI** (optional) – connect Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, a private local Ollama model or your
  installed Claude Code.
  - **Ask AI** explains any finding in plain language: what it means, how dangerous it is, what to do.
  - **Second opinion** rates a skill or MCP server as safe, suspicious or malicious – with reasons.
  - You see the exact text before anything is sent. Secrets are removed first. Keys are kept in your system keychain.
    The AI only advises – ZaRemba never acts on an AI answer alone.
- ⌨️ Command line: `zaremba quarantine list | auto | restore | delete`
- 🔄 Future-proof threat updates: new kinds of threat entries will not interrupt older app versions.

### 🧩 Also new: browser extension (developer preview)
The **Prompt-Injection Shield** for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Firefox is attached to this release:
- 🛡️ Finds instructions hidden in web pages for AI browsers and assistants – and neutralizes them with one click
- 🔑 Stops API keys, private keys and passwords before they are pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and 13 other AI chats
- 🧩 Checks your extensions against 34 fake AI extensions caught stealing chats and e-mails

Install in developer mode: [step-by-step guide](https://github.com/kayzaa/ZaRemba-AI-Watcher-Official/blob/main/docs/BROWSER_EXTENSION.md). Store versions are coming.

Learn more: [Quarantine & bring your own AI](https://github.com/kayzaa/ZaRemba-AI-Watcher-Official/blob/main/docs/QUARANTINE_AND_AI.md)

### ⚠️ Good to know
- The installers are not yet signed with Microsoft/Apple certificates – Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper ask
  once. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/kayzaa/ZaRemba-AI-Watcher-Official/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).
- AI answers can be wrong – treat them as a second opinion. Requests to API providers are billed to your own account.
- Agent Guard integrations other than Claude Code are experimental.
- The browser extension is a developer preview: developer-mode installs do not update themselves, and Firefox keeps it
  only until restart until the Firefox Add-ons version is available.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
